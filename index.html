About Jon

A lead product designer specialising in fintech platforms, design systems and brand. User-centred in approach, with craft firmly at the core.

Two decades of design, from ad campaigns for O2 and Coca-Cola to design systems and trading applications used across global markets. I solve hard problems for users and clients, and make sure the result looks great too.

Based in Richmond, West London, I can usually be found on a road bike somewhere hillier than planned, or sketching in a notebook.
