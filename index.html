About Jon

A lead product designer that has specialised in fintech platforms, design systems and brand cognisance. Always with a user-centred approach, but with craft firmly at the core.

With over two decades designing stuff – from ad campaigns for O2 and Coca-Cola to building design systems and applications for global trading platforms. Solving problems for users and clients, but always making it look awesome.

Based in Richmond, West London, I can usually be found on a road bike somewhere (often hillier than planned) or drawing in my notebook.
